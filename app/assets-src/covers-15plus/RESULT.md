# Cover generation prompts

Each prompt below produced the named final PNG using the built-in image generation tool. `_style-ref.png` was attached as the style reference on every call.

## `a1-glasses-book.png`

```text
Create one original portrait illustration for a teen book cover, 1024 × 1536 pixels, 2:3 aspect ratio. Use the attached _style-ref.png strictly as the approved style reference. Match its refined hand-inked navy (#24364B) contours, restrained fine hatching, subtle grain of warm cream paper (#F9EFE3 / #FBE9D6), translucent watercolour washes and soft blue-teal cast shadows. Do not reproduce the reference hand, wrist or radiating ornaments. Ochre and amber washes provide the main accents.

A pair of round reading glasses with both temples folded rests on an open school reading book whose two pages are completely blank. The book sits on a wooden school desk, with a phone lying face-down beside it, its plain back and small unbranded camera visible.

One gentle, quiet still life, centered on generous plain warm cream paper. Confine all scene elements to the middle 60 percent vertically, with ample plain paper above and below and ample side margins. No object, furniture, floor, window, door or cast shadow touches a canvas edge. Let the painted setting dissolve into the surrounding paper with soft, irregular vignette edges. No decorative frame or border. Paper and screens have no markings except the pencil diagram if specifically described. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, clock digits or UI writing. No people, faces, hands, animals or religious symbols.
```
